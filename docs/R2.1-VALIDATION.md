## 2. Menti visual changes

Fuller, rounded five-point geometry; cyan/electric-blue/violet/magenta gradients; soft internal lighting; small glossy highlights; sparse fixed spark detail; luminous rim and restrained local glow. Larger expressive eyes retain deep irises, clear pupils and star-shaped glossy reflections. Curved brows and a warm closed smile preserve the default expression. The navy cap has separate crown/board shading, cyan/violet edge light and an elegant gold tassel ending in a small star. The cap sits behind the star's upper point rather than concealing it.

Menti width increases from 124 to 160px on desktop, 102 to 132px at 1024px, and 96 to 124px on mobile: approximately 29%. Narrow mobile uses 120px, a 25% increase. The character remains smaller than the learner scene and secondary to the message.

Speech keeps its honest upcoming-guide copy, with softer asymmetric corners, a violet/cyan edge, near-opaque glass and a small pointer toward Menti. It reads as character speech rather than another capability tile.

## 3. Menti component architecture

`Menti` composes independent body, face and cap layers. `MentiFace` owns the default brows/smile and composes `MentiEyes`; `menti-eyes` and `menti-pupils` hooks preserve the existing blink/tracking architecture. Each character instance retains unique gradient IDs, responsive size and body-color CSS variables. Separating the default expression layer makes later expression variants straightforward without introducing a state machine or speculative conversational functionality.

`HeroExperience` is byte-for-byte unchanged. Pointer throttling, bounds, touch gating, pause/resume, media subscriptions and cleanup remain the original R2 implementation. Meet Menti retains the original availability dialog. Hover and keyboard focus provide a small glance, slight dock response, speech emphasis and brightness. Character breathing is slightly gentler; duration, finite iterations and blink timing are preserved.

## 4. Learner-image treatment

The original local learner WebP, image sizing, optimization, alternative text and priority are unchanged. A thin cyan/violet gradient rim, layered frame, restrained shadow, soft background light and selective edge tint integrate the photograph into the scene. The central image remains clear; no card covers the learner's face. No additional image or image-processing dependency was introduced.

## 5. Capability cards

The four concepts, captions, accessible link semantics and exact destinations are unchanged. Cards gain shaded dark glass, an inner highlight, clearer borders, stronger icon presence and semibold title hierarchy. Slightly adjusted asymmetric placement clears the larger guide. Hover/focus edge and icon illumination remain subtle. All four surfaces continue to disable backdrop filtering; mobile keeps its readable two-column grid.

## 6. Atmosphere and pathways

Cyan/violet radial atmosphere stays concentrated around the right-hand scene, with clean left-side reading space. Thin orbital arcs have clearer color, a faint broad aura, fixed illuminated nodes and a subtle static bridge toward the horizon. The original single finite moving highlight is preserved; no animation timer or moving-node system was added. Mobile hides the bridge and extra arcs, reduces stars from ten to four, and lowers local frame/character glow.

## 7. Global horizon

The existing Earth curvature remains. Chart-like gridlines and angular connections are replaced by faint curved land silhouettes, small fixed city-light clusters and a few smooth opportunity arcs, with occasional cyan/gold points. Labels remain decorative ecosystem concepts, covered by the unchanged development disclosure; they are not live opportunity listings.

## 8. CTAs

Explore Programs retains its original link and light/dark contrast, with a refined light surface, cyan edge light and restrained violet secondary glow. Existing arrow feedback remains. Meet Menti uses quieter shaded navy glass and a subtle violet border, with the character reaction emphasized on hover or keyboard focus. No new modal or action was added.

## 9. Mobile and tablet composition

The original breakpoints and narrative structure are preserved. Desktop and landscape tablet retain the layered two-area scene; portrait tablet retains its corrected editorial area above the visual. The absolute desktop guide dock shifts slightly lower to bridge the learner and horizon while maintaining card clearance. Mobile still follows message → CTAs → guide → learner → capability grid. Its larger character adds modest vertical space while preserving readable content and actions.

## 10. Accessibility preserved

Semantic content, heading label, decorative aria treatment, link/button semantics, touch targets, native dialog and navigation behavior are unchanged. Visible keyboard focus remains. Hover reactions have keyboard equivalents. Axe WCAG A/AA checks pass for the page, open Menti availability dialog and existing navigation states across all six test projects. Geometry tests confirm cards remain separate from the heading, CTAs and Menti dock. Automated scans do not establish a complete accessibility certification.

## 11. Reduced motion preserved

The original preference subscription and fallback behavior remain. Reduced motion disables character loops, pupil tracking, moving path highlights, staged entrances and positional hover/focus feedback. Static light, gradients and depth remain. Preference changes reset gaze, and the motion control hides when irrelevant. Pause/resume and no-JavaScript readability remain covered by the existing suite. The newly matched keyboard dock response is also explicitly disabled under reduced motion.

## 12. Performance impact

Additional work is static server-rendered SVG/CSS detail. No new client interaction code, runtime dependency, font, raster asset, WebGL, canvas, video, large-area blur or animation engine was introduced. Gradients replace expensive rendering; glow stays local and modest. Capability cards retain zero backdrop filters, with only the small speech surface retaining its existing 8px blur. The homepage remains statically prerendered. Field Core Web Vitals and real-device network performance were not measured.

Future expression variants and assistant functionality are not implemented. Safari/Firefox, assistive-technology/device review and field performance remain unmeasured. No deployment was performed. R2.1 stops at the hero; Modules and subsequent sections are untouched.
